Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Supernova: The Nexus (“we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information when you use Creator Space (“Website”). Replace the bracketed owner and contact details before publishing this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • TikTok sign-in: when you choose to sign in, we request the user.info.basic scope and may receive your TikTok Open ID, display name, and profile avatar URL.
  • Fan art requests: the signed-in account identifier and display name, your request description, optional reference URL, submission time, and moderation status.
  • Anonymous notes: the alias you enter (or “Anonymous”), note text, submission time, and moderation status. Do not include personal or sensitive information in a note.
  • Business inquiries: name, email address, subject, message, and optionally the signed-in account identifier.
  • Technical data: basic information processed by Google and GitHub when their hosting or platform services are used, subject to their own policies.

2. How we use information

We use information to authenticate users, associate fan art requests with the correct account, review and moderate submissions, respond to inquiries, operate the Website, prevent abuse, and maintain service security.

3. TikTok data

TikTok data is obtained only after you authorize the Login Kit flow. We use the basic profile information described above for account identification and the Website experience. We do not use it to publish content to TikTok, access private TikTok content, sell personal information, or serve targeted advertising through this integration.

4. Storage and service providers

Submission and profile records may be stored in Google Sheets through Google Apps Script. The public Website and static policy pages may be hosted by GitHub Pages. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Keep Apps Script credentials and TikTok client secrets private.

5. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information if required by law, to protect the Website and its users, or to service providers that help operate the Website. Public notes or approved fan art may be displayed on the Website after moderation; avoid submitting information you do not want made public.

6. Retention and deletion

We retain information only as long as reasonably needed to operate the Website, manage submissions, resolve inquiries, meet legal obligations, and protect against abuse. To request access, correction, or deletion of information associated with your submission or account, contact us using the address below. We may need to verify your request before acting.

7. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect stored information. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Do not send passwords, payment card details, or other highly sensitive information through Website forms.

8. Children

The Website is not intended to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review the request.

9. Changes

We may update this policy as the Website changes. The updated date above indicates the latest revision.

10. Contact

For privacy requests or questions, contact mathematicscc@gmail.com.
